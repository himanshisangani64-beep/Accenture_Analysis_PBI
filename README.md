## 📌 Project Overview

The **Accenture – Indian Workforce & HR Analytics Dashboard** (Data As On: **30 April 2025**) is an interactive business intelligence project developed using Microsoft Power BI.

The project transforms employee records into meaningful analytical insights, helping understand workforce growth, employee retention, salary structures, performance ratings, and departmental HR health.

## 🎯 Business Problem & Objectives

Managing a workforce of 50,000+ employees across multiple cities, departments, and job levels creates challenges in monitoring employee retention, compensation, and organizational performance.

**The objective is to develop an interactive HR analytics dashboard that helps answer important business questions.**

- Analyze total and active employee headcount.
- Identify departments and cities with higher workforce concentration.
- Understand employee attrition and retention trends.
- Evaluate salary distribution across departments and job levels.
- Analyze gender-wise compensation distribution.
- Monitor performance ratings and training completion.
- Compare current-year and previous-year HR performance.
